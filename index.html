AI and CyberSecurity:

Role in contributing to cyber defence and cyber crime makes it a Friend or Foe?

How AI facilitates cyber crime?

AI automates the time consuming task cyber criminals requires to conduct an attack. Specifically the reconnaissance stage of information gathering. Previously researching attack surface of targets through OSINT, analysing network vulnerabilities, and creating malicious piece of code can be processed and analysed instantaeneously through AI.

AI deepfakes aids social engineering attacks. Through generating false images, videos and voice phishing, it can make phishing attack much convincing by adapting resource with contextual plausibility to be compelling for victims. Increasing likelihood of victims to provide unauthorised access to threat actors.

AI produces a new attack surface due to the possibility of its system being exploited. Threat actors can instruct AI systems through prompt injection to behave contrary or act maliciously.

How AI supports cyber security?

AI changes vulnerability management by guiding security decisions through depth of information and risk assessment to prioritise risk levels.

AI transforms security operation center by efficiently investigating suspicious activities, analyse security events to detect anomalies, correlate events and identify patterns. By effeciently analysing behavioural patterns across huge datasets it streamline UEBA (User and Entity Behavious Analytics).

AI can help incident response by enabling security researchers to investigate time logs of suspicous activity. It can also accelerate threat hunting by providing valuabe insight on subtle patterns that can easily remain undetected.

AI Security Safeguard

  • Ensuring private data or projects are never shared or discussed with any AI entity.
  • Monitoring AI systems regularly for any vulnerabilities.
  • AI could hallucinate, ensure AI does not replace cyber security judgements.
